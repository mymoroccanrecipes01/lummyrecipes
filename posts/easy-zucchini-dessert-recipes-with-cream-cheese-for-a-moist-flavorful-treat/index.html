Moist Zucchini Cream Cheese Swirl Cake You’ll Want to Bake Again

Lummy Hayes
Published on August 7, 2026
15 min Prep Time
30 min Time
8 Servings
Easy Difficulty

This zucchini cream cheese swirl cake is surprisingly moist and creamy thanks to fresh zucchini and a luscious cream cheese filling. It’s perfect for a cozy afternoon snack or sharing with friends at your next casual gathering.

Introduction

I first stumbled upon this zucchini cream cheese swirl cake recipe when I had an abundance of zucchini from my garden and wasn't quite sure what to do with it. I wanted something that wasn’t just a basic zucchini bread, so I played around with adding a luscious cream cheese swirl. The result was a delightfully moist and creamy cake that felt a bit special but was still easy to whip up. What makes this version stand out is how the cream cheese filling adds a tangy richness that perfectly balances the subtle earthiness of the zucchini. I also learned that squeezing out the zucchini well before mixing is key to avoiding a soggy texture. The finished cake is tender, with just the right amount of swirl to make every bite interesting. It’s become my go-to for afternoon snacks or when friends pop by unexpectedly. Honestly, I think this cake tastes even better the next day, once the flavors have melded together. If you’re looking for a cozy, homemade treat that’s a little different but still comforting, you’ll love this one.

What You'll Need

One 9x5 inch loaf, 8 servings

  • 2 cups grated fresh zucchini, squeezed dry
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/4 cup granulated sugar (for cream cheese filling)
  • 1 large egg yolk (for cream cheese filling)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (for cream cheese filling)

How To Do It

Step 1
Start by grating your zucchini finely, then place it in a clean kitchen towel and squeeze out as much moisture as possible. This step is crucial because zucchini holds a lot of water, and if you skip squeezing, your cake will be soggy and won’t hold its shape well. The drier the zucchini, the better the texture of your cake will be, so be thorough here.
⏱ Time: 10 minutes
✓ Doneness: Zucchini feels dry to the touch and doesn’t release water when pressed
Step 2
In a large bowl, whisk together the sugar, vegetable oil, eggs, and vanilla extract until the mixture is smooth and slightly thickened. This will help incorporate air and ensure your cake is tender and moist. Don’t rush this step; a well-mixed batter makes a difference in the final texture.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Batter is smooth, pale, and slightly glossy
Step 3
In a separate bowl, sift together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Sifting helps distribute the leavening agents evenly, which is important for a uniform rise in your cake. Mixing dry ingredients separately also prevents overmixing when combined with wet ingredients later.
⏱ Time: 3 minutes
✓ Doneness: Dry ingredients are well combined and lump-free
Step 4
Add the dry ingredients gradually to the wet mixture, folding gently with a spatula just until combined. Overmixing can develop gluten and make the cake tough, so stop mixing as soon as you see no streaks of flour. Then fold in the grated zucchini carefully, ensuring it’s evenly distributed without breaking down the batter.
⏱ Time: 4 minutes
✓ Doneness: Batter is thick, moist, and uniform with zucchini evenly dispersed
Step 5
To make the cream cheese swirl, beat the softened cream cheese with sugar, egg yolk, and vanilla extract until smooth and creamy. This mixture adds a rich tanginess and creamy texture that complements the zucchini perfectly. Make sure your cream cheese is soft to avoid lumps and achieve a smooth swirl.
⏱ Time: 5 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cream cheese filling is smooth, glossy, and lump-free
Step 6
Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and grease a 9x5 inch loaf pan well. Pour half of the zucchini batter into the pan and spread it evenly. Dollop spoonfuls of the cream cheese filling over this layer, then pour the remaining batter on top. Using a knife or skewer, gently swirl the cream cheese through the batter to create a marbled effect. Be careful not to over-swirl or the layers will blend too much.
⏱ Time: 7 minutes
✓ Doneness: Swirl pattern is visible but not completely mixed
Step 7
Bake the cake in the preheated oven for about 30-35 minutes. Check for doneness by inserting a toothpick into the center; it should come out mostly clean with a few moist crumbs attached. Baking too long can dry out the cake, so start checking at 30 minutes. The edges should be golden and slightly pulling away from the pan.
⏱ Time: 30-35 minutes
✓ Doneness: Toothpick inserted into center comes out with moist crumbs, edges golden
Step 8
Once baked, remove the cake from the oven and let it cool in the pan for about 10 minutes. This resting time helps the cake firm up, making it easier to slice without crumbling. Then transfer the cake to a wire rack to cool completely. Cooling completely enhances the flavors and texture.
⏱ Time: 15 minutes
✓ Doneness: Cake is firm to the touch and cool enough to slice cleanly
Step 9
Slice and serve the zucchini cream cheese swirl cake at room temperature. It pairs wonderfully with a cup of tea or coffee. If you want, you can dust it lightly with powdered sugar for a pretty finish. Leftovers keep well wrapped tightly in the fridge.
⏱ Time: 0 minutes
✓ Doneness: Slices hold shape and cream cheese swirl is clearly visible

Pro Tips

Make sure to squeeze the zucchini really well before adding it to the batter.
Removing excess moisture prevents a soggy cake and helps the batter hold together properly. If you skip this, the cake can become dense and fail to rise well.
Use room temperature cream cheese for the swirl filling.
Softened cream cheese blends smoothly into the filling, avoiding lumps and ensuring a creamy texture. Cold cream cheese can cause uneven mixing and clumps.
Fold dry ingredients gently into the wet mixture without overmixing.
Overmixing develops gluten in the flour, which toughens the cake. Gentle folding keeps the crumb tender and light.
Don’t over-swirl the cream cheese into the batter.
Swirling too much blends the filling fully into the batter, losing the beautiful marbled effect and distinct flavor pockets.
Check the cake starting at 30 minutes with a toothpick.
Ovens vary, and overbaking dries the cake. A toothpick with moist crumbs signals perfect doneness.
Cool the cake completely before slicing.
This helps the cake set and prevents crumbling, making neat slices and enhancing flavor melding.
Use a sharp serrated knife to slice the cake.
A serrated knife cuts through the moist crumb and cream cheese swirl cleanly without squashing the cake.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

Not squeezing out the zucchini moisture

Why it happens Many don’t realize zucchini holds a surprising amount of water, assuming grating is enough.

Fix After grating, place zucchini in a clean towel and wring firmly to remove as much liquid as possible. This ensures the batter isn’t too wet and the cake bakes properly.

Overmixing the batter

Why it happens It’s tempting to mix thoroughly to avoid lumps, but this activates gluten in flour.

Fix Mix just until dry ingredients disappear. Folding gently keeps the cake tender and prevents toughness.

Using cold cream cheese for the filling

Why it happens Sometimes cream cheese is added straight from the fridge, causing lumps.

Fix Soften cream cheese at room temperature before mixing to ensure a smooth, creamy swirl.

Overbaking the cake

Why it happens Leaving the cake in too long makes it dry and crumbly.

Fix Start checking at 30 minutes with a toothpick. Remove when it comes out with moist crumbs, not completely dry.

Swirling cream cheese too much

Why it happens Trying to get an even swirl can lead to over-mixing.

Fix Use a gentle swirling motion just a few times to create a marbled look and keep distinct pockets of cream cheese.

Variations

Add walnuts for crunch

Fold in 1/2 cup chopped walnuts to the batter before baking. This adds a lovely nutty crunch and texture contrast to the creamy cake. The walnuts also complement the earthiness of the zucchini nicely.

Note: Chop walnuts evenly to ensure they cook through and don’t sink to the bottom.

Swap cream cheese with mascarpone

Use mascarpone instead of cream cheese for a richer, silkier swirl. This variation creates a more delicate flavor and a smoother texture that melts in your mouth.

Note: Mascarpone is softer, so be gentle when swirling to avoid overmixing.

Make it gluten-free

Replace all-purpose flour with a 1:1 gluten-free baking blend. This makes the cake suitable for gluten sensitivities without sacrificing tenderness or rise.

Note: Check your gluten-free mix contains xanthan gum or add 1/4 tsp for structure.

Add cinnamon and nutmeg

Spice up the batter with 1 tsp cinnamon and 1/4 tsp nutmeg for warm, cozy flavors that pair beautifully with zucchini and cream cheese.

Note: Add spices to the dry ingredients to ensure even distribution.

Use brown sugar instead of white

Swap granulated sugar for brown sugar in the batter for a deeper, caramel-like sweetness and moister crumb. This gives the cake a richer flavor profile.

Note: Brown sugar may slightly darken the batter and increase moisture.

Storage & Tips

Room Temperature

This cake keeps well at room temperature for up to 2 days if covered tightly with plastic wrap or stored in a cake dome. Keeping it sealed prevents it from drying out and preserves moisture.

Refrigerator

Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days. The cream cheese swirl means refrigeration is recommended for freshness. The cake may firm up when chilled; bring to room temperature before serving for best texture.

Freezer

Wrap the cake tightly in plastic wrap and then foil to freeze for up to 2 months. Freeze in slices for convenience. Thaw overnight in the fridge and then bring to room temperature before serving to restore softness.

Reheating

Warm slices gently in a microwave for 15-20 seconds or in a 300°F oven for 5-7 minutes. Avoid overheating which can dry the cake. A light brush of butter or a splash of milk before reheating can help maintain moisture.

Make Ahead

You can prepare the batter and cream cheese filling a day ahead and keep them refrigerated separately. Assemble and bake the next day for fresher texture. Alternatively, bake the cake a day in advance and store wrapped to let flavors meld.

FAQ

Can I use frozen zucchini for this cake?
You can use frozen zucchini, but make sure to thaw it completely and squeeze out all excess moisture very well. Frozen zucchini tends to be wetter, so extra care is needed to avoid a soggy cake. Fresh zucchini usually gives the best texture.
What can I substitute for cream cheese in the swirl?
You can try mascarpone or ricotta for a similar creamy texture, though flavor will vary slightly. Greek yogurt mixed with a bit of cream cheese can also work. Avoid using low-fat cream cheese as it may not swirl properly.
How do I know when the cake is fully baked?
Insert a toothpick into the center; it should come out with a few moist crumbs but no wet batter. The edges will be golden and starting to pull away from the pan. Baking times can vary, so start checking at 30 minutes.
Can I make this cake vegan?
This recipe relies on eggs and cream cheese, so making it fully vegan requires substitutions. You could try flax eggs and vegan cream cheese alternatives, but texture and flavor will differ. Experimentation is needed for best results.
What if my batter seems too dry or too wet?
If too dry, add a splash of milk or oil to loosen it slightly. If too wet, check your zucchini moisture and try squeezing more liquid out next time. The batter should be thick but pourable for best results.
How do I store leftover cake?
Wrap leftovers tightly in plastic wrap or place in an airtight container. Store in the fridge due to the cream cheese swirl, and consume within 5 days for best flavor and safety.
Can I double this recipe?
Yes! Double the ingredients and bake in two loaf pans or a larger pan. Adjust baking time accordingly and check doneness carefully, as larger pans may need longer baking.
Will this cake freeze well?
Absolutely. Wrap tightly in plastic and foil to prevent freezer burn. Freeze up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge and warm gently before serving for best texture.
